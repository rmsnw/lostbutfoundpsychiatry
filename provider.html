I'm Theonie Labee-Walker, a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner with a passion for helping individuals navigate life's most difficult emotional and psychological challenges.

With more than a decade of healthcare experience, including working with individuals affected by trauma, PTSD, depression, and anxiety, I understand that mental health challenges can affect every aspect of a person's life.

My approach is rooted in compassion, respect, and the belief that everyone deserves to feel seen, heard, and understood. I provide comprehensive psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and supportive, evidence-based interventions tailored to each individual's needs.

I believe healing is not about forgetting where you've been. It's about discovering that your past does not determine where you can go.

Whether you're struggling with anxiety, depression, trauma, ADHD, or overwhelming life circumstances, my goal is to provide a welcoming environment where we can work together toward meaningful and lasting improvement. You don't have to navigate your mental health journey alone.

Working Together

What you can expect

A thorough first conversation

Your first visit is a full psychiatric consultation, giving Theonie time to understand your history, symptoms and goals before recommending next steps.

A plan built around you

No two people are the same, so your care is personalized around your goals, values and lifestyle rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Ongoing, responsive support

Regular check-ins mean your plan, including any medication, can be adjusted as your needs change over time.

A judgment-free space

Every conversation with Theonie is treated with confidentiality, respect and compassion. Always, without exception.
