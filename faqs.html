01Getting Started

Do you offer telehealth appointments?

Yes. Both secure telehealth and in-person appointments are available. Whether you prefer the convenience of meeting from home or feel more comfortable connecting in person, you can choose the option that works best for you.

What is a psychiatric consultation?

It’s an opportunity for me to understand what you’ve been experiencing, what may be contributing to your symptoms, and what you hope to change. I'll review your mental health and medical history, discuss your concerns, and work together to create a treatment plan that feels appropriate for your needs, goals, and lifestyle.

Do you provide psychiatry as well as therapy?

My primary focus is psychiatric evaluation and medication management, with supportive therapy incorporated when appropriate. If you need ongoing psychotherapy, I’m happy to collaborate with your therapist or help connect you with additional resources.

Who do you see for appointments?

I welcome patients ages 12 and older from diverse backgrounds and life experiences. I provide care for depression, anxiety, ADHD, trauma and PTSD, mood disorders, and other mental health concerns. Treatment is compassionate, collaborative, and personalized to your needs.

02Appointments & Visits

How do I schedule my first appointment?

You can request an appointment online or contact the office directly. Once you're scheduled, you'll receive everything you need to prepare for your first visit.

Can you prescribe medication?

Yes. Medication may be recommended as part of your treatment when appropriate. We’ll discuss the potential benefits, risks, alternatives, and any concerns you may have so that you can make an informed decision about your care.

What should I expect on my first visit?

Your first visit is a 60-minute comprehensive psychiatric evaluation where we’ll discuss your current concerns, history, and treatment goals. Together, we’ll explore your options and create a personalized treatment plan that meets your needs.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice and missed appointments may be subject to a $50 fee.

03Insurance & Billing

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. I accept several insurance plans, which are listed on the Insurance section of the website. Please confirm your coverage and benefits with your insurance provider before your first appointment.

What if I do not have insurance?

Self-pay options are available. The initial 60-minute psychiatric evaluation is $250, and standard follow-up appointments are $125.

What forms of payment do you accept?

I accept cash and major credit and debit cards. Payment is due at the time of service.

04Privacy & Records

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your privacy is important to me. Your health information is protected in accordance with HIPAA and applicable privacy laws. Please avoid sending sensitive medical information through regular email, text messages, or website forms.

Where can I find patient forms?

Patient forms are available on the Forms page. New patients can download and complete the required forms before their first appointment.

Can I use this website in an emergency?

No. This website is not monitored for emergencies. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Still have questions?

We are happy to talk through anything not covered here.
