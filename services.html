Services

Behavioral Health Services

Including medication management and supportive psychotherapy, so you know exactly how we can help before you ever call.

What We Offer

Care for the mind, in the way that works for you

Every service below can be delivered telehealth or in person, with insurance accepted.

Psychiatric Evaluations

Comprehensive assessments and individualized treatment planning.

Medication Management

Evidence-based medication treatment and ongoing monitoring.

Depression Treatment

Support for persistent sadness, low motivation, and mood changes.

Anxiety Treatment

Treatment for excessive worry, panic, and emotional distress.

ADHD Treatment

Evaluation and management of attention and executive-functioning difficulties.

Trauma & PTSD

Compassionate, trauma-informed psychiatric care.

Mood Disorders

Evaluation and treatment of mood instability.

Supportive Psychotherapy

Therapeutic interventions incorporated into psychiatric care when appropriate.

Please Note

Services outside our scope

I Once Was Lost, But Now I'm Found, PLLC does not provide care or evaluations for the following:

  • Psychotic disorders, including active psychosis
  • Acute psychiatric crises
  • Disability claims or evaluations
  • Labor & Industries (L&I) claims or evaluations
  • High-risk substance use disorder detoxification
  • Severe eating disorders
  • Court-ordered legal competency evaluations

These needs require a different setting or specialized service. For immediate help, see our crisis support resources. If you are unsure whether our practice fits your needs, please contact us before scheduling.

How treatment works
