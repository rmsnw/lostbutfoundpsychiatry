Services

Psychiatry & Behavioral Health Services

Psychiatric consultation, medication management, and behavioral health care, built around your goals and available online or in person.

What We Offer

Care for the mind, in the way that works for you

Every service below can be delivered online or in person, with insurance accepted.

Psychiatry Services

Behavioral health care that covers both psychiatry and therapy, all in one practice, so you are not left coordinating between separate providers for medication and talk therapy.

Mental Health Services

Compassionate support for anxiety, depression, trauma, mood disorders and more, delivered in plain language and tailored to your situation, not a generic script.

Psychiatric Consultation

A thorough initial evaluation to understand your history, symptoms and goals, so we can plan next steps together and set realistic expectations from day one.

Online or In-Person

Meet from the comfort of home over secure video, or visit the office in Tacoma. You can choose the format that fits your schedule for each visit.

Prescription of Medicine

When medication is the right choice for you, we handle evaluation, prescribing and ongoing management, with regular check-ins to adjust your plan as needed.

Insurance Accepted

We accept insurance so that quality psychiatric care is within reach. Call the office to confirm your specific plan before your first visit.
