Theonie Labee is a psychiatric nurse practitioner providing psychiatry and behavioral health care at I Once Was Lost, But Now I'm Found, PLLC in Tacoma, Washington.

As a Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner, Theonie offers psychiatric consultation, prescription of medicine when it is appropriate, and support that covers both psychiatry and therapy, so you can receive care for your mind in one trusted place.

Her approach is compassionate and evidence-based. Every plan is personalized around your goals, values and lifestyle, and grounded in the belief that healing includes the whole person: mind, body and spirit. Care is confidential, inclusive, and judgment-free. Always.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't find what you are looking for? Call our office and we will be glad to help.

Contact us
Do you offer online appointments?

Yes. Appointments are available online or in person, whichever fits your life best.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, insurance is accepted. Please call our office to confirm that your plan is covered before your first visit.

Can you prescribe medication?

Yes. Prescription of medicine is part of our psychiatric care when it is appropriate for you. Decisions are made together during your consultation.

What is a psychiatric consultation?

It is a conversation about what you are experiencing and what you hope for, so that a personalized plan can be created around your goals, values and lifestyle.

Do you provide therapy as well as psychiatry?

Our behavioral health care covers both psychiatry and therapy.

Where can I find patient forms?

Patient forms are available on this page as downloadable PDFs. They cannot be filled out online, so please print them and bring them to your appointment. Go to forms.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We protect your health information in accordance with HIPAA. Please avoid sending sensitive medical information by regular email, text message or website forms. Read our HIPAA notice.

Can I use this website in an emergency?

No. This website is for informational and administrative purposes only. If you are experiencing a medical or mental health emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

HIPAA Information

HIPAA Privacy and Confidentiality

Our practice is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of your personal and protected health information in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and other applicable federal and state privacy laws.

01

Protected Health Information

Any health, medical, psychiatric, billing, or identifying information you provide to our practice may be considered Protected Health Information (PHI). We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect this information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

02

Secure Communication

Information submitted through this website, patient portals, secure electronic forms, or other approved communication methods will be handled in accordance with applicable privacy and security requirements. However, standard email, text messaging, and general website contact forms may not always provide the same level of security as HIPAA-compliant communication systems. Please avoid submitting highly sensitive or confidential medical information through unsecured communication methods unless specifically instructed by our office.

03

How Your Information Is Used

Your health information will not be used or disclosed except as permitted or required by law, for purposes related to treatment, payment, healthcare operations, or with your written authorization when required.

04

Your Privacy Rights

For additional information regarding how your health information may be used or disclosed, your privacy rights, or our privacy practices, please review our Notice of Privacy Practices or contact our office directly.
