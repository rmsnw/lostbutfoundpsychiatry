Our Mission

Mental health care that meets you where you are

I Once Was Lost, But Now I'm Found, PLLC offers psychiatry services and behavioral health care, covering both psychiatry and therapy, in Tacoma, Washington. Visits are available online or in person, so you can get support in whatever way fits your life.

Our name reflects what we believe: no one is defined by their lowest moment. Whatever brought you here, whether it is anxiety, depression, trauma, a mood disorder, or simply the sense that something needs to change, we are here to help you find your way back to yourself.

  • Confidential, inclusive, and judgment-free care
  • Personalized plans built around your goals, values and lifestyle
  • Insurance accepted, with online and in-person appointments

How We Practice

Our approach to care

Four principles guide every appointment, from your very first consultation onward.

Evidence-based care

We draw on proven, research-backed treatments for anxiety, depression, trauma, mood disorders and more, rather than one-size-fits-all approaches.

Personalized to you

No two people are the same, so no two treatment plans should be either. We build every plan around your goals, values and lifestyle.

Whole-person support

Lasting change considers mind, body and spirit together, not just a list of symptoms. We take a holistic view of your well-being.

A safe, judgment-free space

Every conversation is treated with confidentiality, respect and compassion. Always, without exception.
