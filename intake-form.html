Consent for Psychiatric Evaluation & Treatment

Psychiatric Medication Treatment Consent

I understand that psychiatric medications may be recommended as part of treatment. I will have an opportunity to discuss the reason for treatment, expected benefits, significant risks and side effects, reasonable alternatives, and questions I may have.

I agree to provide accurate information about prescription and nonprescription medications, supplements, alcohol, cannabis or other substances when clinically relevant; medication allergies; significant medical changes; and medications prescribed by other clinicians.

When relevant to medication safety, I agree to discuss pregnancy, suspected pregnancy, breastfeeding, or plans for pregnancy with my provider.

I understand that I should not change a prescribed dose or abruptly stop medication without discussing it with my provider when reasonably possible. Some medications may cause withdrawal symptoms or other adverse effects when stopped suddenly. Medication response varies, and no specific result is guaranteed.

Notice of Privacy Practices

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

Your Information. Your Rights. Our Responsibilities.

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed, how you can obtain access to that information, and the privacy responsibilities of I Once Was Lost, But Now I'm Found, PLLC.

Your Rights

  • Request access to or a copy of your paper or electronic health record, subject to applicable law.
  • Ask us to correct or amend information in your record.
  • Request confidential communications.
  • Ask us to limit certain uses or disclosures. Some requested restrictions are not required to be accepted, except where law requires otherwise.
  • Request an accounting of certain disclosures.
  • Receive a paper copy of this Notice.
  • Choose an authorized personal representative to act for you.
  • File a privacy complaint without retaliation.

Your Choices

In circumstances where your permission is required, we will obtain it before using or disclosing your information. You may have choices regarding communication with family or others involved in your care and certain other disclosures.

Our Uses and Disclosures

We may use or disclose health information as permitted or required by law, including for treatment, payment, health care operations, public health and safety activities, legal requirements, and other purposes authorized by law. Washington law may provide additional protections for mental health information. Psychotherapy notes, when maintained separately as defined by law, receive additional protection.

Substance Use Disorder Records

Federal law provides additional confidentiality protections for certain substance use disorder patient records. Where those protections apply, the practice will use and disclose such records in accordance with applicable federal law.

Our Responsibilities

  • We are required by law to maintain the privacy and security of protected health information.
  • We will notify affected individuals as required if a breach compromises unsecured protected information.
  • We must follow the duties and privacy practices described in the Notice currently in effect.
  • We will not use or disclose information other than as permitted by law or described in the Notice unless you authorize us in writing.

Patient Rights & Responsibilities

You have the right to be treated with dignity, privacy, respect, and without unlawful discrimination; to participate in treatment decisions; to ask questions; to discuss reasonable alternatives; and to decline treatment to the extent permitted by law.

As a participant in your care, please:

  • Provide accurate and complete health, medication, pharmacy, and contact information.
  • Ask questions when you do not understand your treatment plan.
  • Inform your provider of significant changes in symptoms, medications, health, or safety.
  • Attend scheduled appointments or provide appropriate notice when unable to attend.
  • Use medications according to the agreed treatment plan and discuss concerns promptly.
  • Treat staff and others respectfully and participate in treatment decisions.

Consent for AI-Assisted Clinical Documentation (Twofold Health)

Purpose

To allow your provider to focus more fully on you and the conversation during your appointment rather than primarily on typing or taking notes, I Once Was Lost, But Now I'm Found, PLLC may use Twofold Health, an artificial intelligence (AI)-assisted clinical documentation tool, with your permission.

How It Is Used

During an appointment, Twofold may process audio from the conversation to help create a transcript and draft clinical documentation. According to Twofold Health, encounter audio is processed for documentation and is not retained as a session recording after processing. Twofold also states that patient information is not used to train its AI models.

The AI-generated documentation is a draft only. Your psychiatric provider remains responsible for reviewing, correcting, and approving the final clinical note. Twofold does not independently diagnose you, prescribe medication, or make treatment decisions.

Privacy

Information processed for clinical documentation may include protected health information (PHI). The practice will use the service in accordance with applicable privacy and security requirements and its agreement with the technology provider. The final clinical documentation may become part of your medical record.

Your Choice

Use of AI-assisted documentation is voluntary. You may decline without affecting your ability to receive care, the quality of your treatment, or your relationship with your provider. Even if you consent, you may ask that Twofold not be used during a particular visit or withdraw permission for future visits.
