Before Your Visit

What to expect

To make the most of your first appointment, we ask new patients to complete a short set of forms in advance. Forms are provided as PDF documents to download and print. They cannot be filled out online, so please bring your completed forms with you to your appointment, or ask our office about secure ways to return them ahead of time.

Patient Forms

Forms to download

New Patient Intake Form

Basic contact information, health history and the reason for your visit. Completing this ahead of time helps your first consultation go smoothly.

Consent for Treatment & Telehealth

Your written consent to receive psychiatric care, including care delivered by secure video for online appointments.

HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices (Acknowledgment)

Confirms you have received and reviewed our Notice of Privacy Practices, explaining how your health information is used and protected.

Insurance & Billing Authorization

Authorizes our office to bill your insurance plan directly and outlines your financial responsibility for any remaining balance.

Release of Information

Needed only if you would like us to share records with, or receive records from, another provider.

Submitting Your Forms

How to return completed forms

Please avoid sending completed forms by regular email, text message, or an unsecured web form, since they may contain protected health information. Bring printed, completed forms with you to your appointment, or call our office and we will let you know about any secure options available to you.

Questions about your paperwork?

Call our office and we will walk you through it.
