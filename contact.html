Before You Reach Out

A few things that help

Have your insurance ready

Keep your insurance card handy so we can quickly confirm whether your plan is accepted.

Know your availability

Think about a few days and times that generally work for you, for either an online or in-person visit.

Jot down your questions

Write down anything you want to ask, whether about the process, pricing, or what to expect.

Keep sensitive details for the call

Avoid sharing detailed medical information by email or text. We will let you know how to share it securely.

Please avoid sending sensitive medical information by regular email, text, or web forms. Call the office, and we will let you know how to share it securely. This site is not for emergencies: call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. For crisis support, call or text 988.
